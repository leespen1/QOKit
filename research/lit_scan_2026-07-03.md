### D. Homogeneity evidence and cost-distribution-only analysis → "Background and related work" + Sec. III remark + Discussion

## Priority threats

1. **kruger2025** — Krüger, Mauerer, "Out of the Loop: Structural Approximation of Optimisation Landscapes and non-Iterative Quantum Optimisation," Quantum 9, 1903 (2025); arXiv:2408.06493. **Nearest published neighbor.** Approximates the expected p=1 QAOA landscape from Hamming-distance solution-space structure and proves a variance-controlled error bound (|E−Ẽ| ≤ sqrt(Var(|T|)·Var(|c_k|²)) — verified from their text). Honest overlap: variance-controlled error bound for a structure-derived classical landscape. Real differentiators: p=1 only; two-level (binary-cost) Hamiltonians only; landscape-level, not state-level; no projection/compression identity; no per-layer telescoping; variance over lightcone-set sizes, not within-cost-class amplitudes. Must cite in Background and contrast in Sec. IV.
2. **burgholzer2025** (bibtex in Must add C) — Lumpability-adjacent bisimulation reduction is *already applied to quantum circuits and benchmarked on QAOA MaxCut/SAT*. The draft's sentence "to our knowledge neither had been connected to the homogeneous proxy" survives only in that exact scoping — do not let it read as "lumpability never connected to quantum circuits." Their reductions are exact-only, with no a posteriori error calculus; claim as new: proxy = PU-iteration identity, telescoping bound for *approximate* lumping, variance identity.
3. **lubich2008 / zoufal2023 / martinazzo2020** (bibtex above) — Theorem 2's *technique* (accumulated projection residuals, unitarity + triangle + induction) is a named theorem in quantum dynamics (Lubich Thm. II.1.5) and has been ported to variational quantum time evolution. Present Theorem 2 as a discrete-time analogue whose value is the *object* it bounds (fixed cost-class frame → exact variance identity → measurable per-layer leakage), not the bounding technique. A numerical-analysis referee will raise Lubich by name.
4. **boulebnane2021** — Boulebnane, Montanaro, "Predicting parameters for the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm for MAX-CUT from the infinite-size limit," arXiv:2110.10685 (preprint). Density-indexed parameter mapping ER(d)→SK: optimal parameter *values* scaling with density is known physics, which is exactly the observed "decalibration." Cite it there and scope the paper's density claim to *compression error at matched schedules*. Also answers "why a proxy on ER at all?" — because no such limit formula exists for BA/WS/real-world families.
5. **Density prior art** (herrman2021, wybo2025; bibtex in Must add E) — density→QAOA-performance correlations exist at n≤8 (exhaustive) and d≤100 (regular, infinite-size). The claim "density, not ER-ness, drives *compression error*" is not preempted, but the abstract/Sec. V wording should acknowledge these and pivot on error-of-compression vs achievable-performance.
6. **mukherjee2026** — Mukherjee et al., "Hamiltonian-Guided Leverage Embedding: Robust Subspace Compression for Efficient QAOA Parameter Estimation," arXiv:2606.07814 (2026, preprint). *Title collision only*: their "subspace compression" is leverage-score sketching of classical feature matrices — no state projection, no N(c';d,c), no Sud citation. Consider a one-line footnote to disambiguate the phrase.

**Overall novelty verdict** (from a Semantic Scholar scan of all 23 works citing Sud et al. through 2026-06, an arXiv author scan of James Sud's 20 papers 2023–2026, and targeted phrase searches for "Perfect Homogeneity"/"homogeneous proxy"): no prior work characterizes the proxy as an orthogonal projection/compression, proves telescoping leakage bounds for it, or derives the within-class variance identity. Mori–Zwanzig applied to quantum circuits/algorithms: clean negative. "Classical shadows of QAOA landscapes": no such paper exists. Contributions (1)–(4) appear open as of today.
